# Top Up Your Wallet

From the wallet page, click **"+ Add balance"** to open the top-up page.

## Steps

1. Enter the desired **amount** in **Rial**, or choose one of the suggested amounts.
2. Choose a **payment gateway**.
3. Click **"Pay"** to be redirected to the bank gateway.
4. After a successful payment, you'll return to the console and your balance will be updated.

## Minimum amount

:::warning
The minimum top-up amount is **200,000 Rial** (equal to 20,000 Toman). The pay button stays disabled for smaller amounts.
:::

You can also enter the amount using Persian digits; it's converted automatically.

## Failed payment

If the payment fails, the result page shows an error message.

:::note Amount deducted but not credited
If the amount was deducted from your account but your wallet balance wasn't increased, don't worry — it will be **automatically refunded within the next 24 hours**.

If the amount hasn't been returned after 24 hours, follow up with a [support ticket](https://console.novin.cloud/dashboard/tickets) and include the transaction's **tracking code** in the ticket.
:::

## Tracking code

The tracking code for each payment is shown in the **Top-up** tab of the wallet page, in the "Tracking code" column. Provide this code to support for any financial issue.
